## 2. Top-10 phenomena (draft ranking, before native check)

1. No plural after numerals: *Uchta kitob / \*kitoblar oldim*
2. Person agreement with an overt pronoun: *Men … kelaman / \*kelasan*
3. Possessive agreement: *Mening akam / \*akang*
4. *hech* + negation, two designs (polarity flip; inserting *hech*)
5. Lexical case, transitive: *itdan / \*itni qoʻrqadi*
6. Anaphor agreement: *oʻzimiz / \*oʻzingiz*
7. Binding: *oʻzimni / \*meni koʻrdim*
8. Case after postpositions: *beri* (safe), *keyin* (adverb reading can rescue it)
9. Ditransitive dative: *oʻquvchilarga / \*oʻquvchilarni*
10. q→gʻ stem change: *oʻrtogʻim / \*oʻrtoqim* (the one phenomenon where the tested letter changes across alphabets)

## 3. Decisions I need

1. **Frame the paper as a benchmark or as a research question?**
   - Benchmark: a TurBLiMP-style resource, about 1,000 pairs.
   - Research question: robustness across alphabets.
   - *My lean (Claude):* a benchmark with the alphabet comparison as its main experiment, since that's what makes it more than "TurBLiMP for Uzbek". To decide with Yana.
2. **Keep the alphabet comparison in, or cut it?**
   - Latin vs Cyrillic looks clean: the script-matched Goldfish models have no `<unk>`.
   - Reformed Latin is confounded by tokenizer coverage.
   - Options: keep all three but only for byte-level models and report `<unk>` rates; keep Latin vs Cyrillic as the main comparison and reformed as a small extra; or cut reformed.
   - Also: Latin→Cyrillic isn't fully rule-based, so every converted sentence needs a human check.
3. **Which phenomena first?**
   - The top 3–5 above are clean and frequent in the corpus.
   - Ask whether to start with phenomena that match TurBLiMP (comparable results) or Uzbek-specific ones (novelty), and how many pairs per phenomenon (≈50–100 if the total is ~1,000).
4. **Can we involve Uzbek linguists, and how should credit work?**
   - Roles: checking the phenomena and pairs; a human judgment study (TurBLiMP used 30 speakers, a 7-point scale and a consent form).
   - Credit: co-authorship for substantial design or validation work vs acknowledgment for annotation. Agree this before inviting anyone.
   - Does a judgment study need ethics approval at Göttingen?
5. **Is it okay to build on TurkicNLP and contribute the tools back?**
   - Pros: Uzbek support, transliteration, a morpheme segmenter with usable ablative/negation labels, Apache-2.0, actively maintained.
   - Cons: pre-alpha, several bugs found today, no reformed alphabet.
   - Proposal: pin the version, verify every output, and start with small bug-fix contributions before proposing the minimal-pair component (sketch in `notes/turkicnlp.md`).
   - Ask how this affects authorship and whether to contact Sherzod Hakimov now.
